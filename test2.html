Introduction to Niet

Noida Institute of Engineering and Technology (NIET), located in Greater Noida, Uttar Pradesh, is a premier self-financed engineering and management institute. Established in 2001 by the City Educational & Social Welfare Society, it is recognized as the first private institute in Uttar Pradesh to be granted Autonomous status by the University Grants Commission (UGC). The institute is spread across a lush 23-acre campus in the regional educational hub of Knowledge Park.

Key Features



  • Accreditation: Re-accredited with a NAAC A Grade (Score: 3.23). Its core programs (including B.Tech CSE, ECE, ME, IT, Biotechnology, B.Pharm, MBA, and MCA) are accredited by the National Board of Accreditation (NBA).
  • Affiliation: While it designs its own industry-focused curriculum due to its autonomous status, the institute remains affiliated with Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam Technical University (AKTU), Lucknow.
  • National Rankings:Consistently ranked by the National Institutional Ranking Framework (NIRF), routinely placing in the 101–150 rank band for Engineering and securing a top 40 spot nationally for its Pharmacy program.



Courses Offered


  • Engineering (B.Tech & M.Tech):Covers traditional streams along with modern, high-demand specializations like Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning (AI/ML), Data Science, Cyber Security, Cloud Computing, and Internet of Things (IoT).
  • Computer Applications & Management:Offers BCA, MCA, BBA, MBA, and PGDM programs.
  • Pharmacy:Delivers B.Pharm and M.Pharm degrees.
  • Unique Curriculums:The institute hosts an International Twinning Program for global exposure and collaborates directly with corporations—such as a specialized B.Tech in Computer Science and Business Systems (CSBS) co-designed with Tata Consultancy Services (TCS).



Infrastructure & Corporate Tie-ups


  • The campus is equipped with specialized Centers of Excellence and Innovation Labs partnered with global tech giants. These include tie-ups with Mercedes-Benz (ADAM Lab), AWS, Cisco, Capgemini, HCLTech, and Oracle. It also features smart classrooms, a 14,000 sq. ft. digital library, separate on-campus hostels, modern sports fields, and a student innovation incubation ecosystem.



Placements


  • Average Package:~₹6.00 LPA
  • Highest Package:Ranges up to ₹44 LPA to ₹51 LPA nationally.
  • Top Recruiters:Regular campus visits from marquee companies including Microsoft, Amazon, Capgemini, TCS, Wipro, and Cognizant.



VISION


  • To be an institute of academic excellence in digital arena with global outreach delivering socially responsible professionals to become a university and an entrepreneurial hub



MISSION


  • To impart quality education and hone students' skills and competencies making them future ready.
  • To foster an ecosystem for research, product development, innovation, incubation, and entrepreneurship.
  • To instill values and ethics to produce socially responsible technocrats addressing global problems.
  • To develop an environment for sharing and exchange of resources globally for lifelong learning.
